Fleebee Statement Downloader

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 5, 2026

Fleebee Statement Downloader is a private utility operated by the Gmail account owner. It uses read-only Gmail access to find specifically labeled bank-statement emails and download their PDF attachments to computers controlled by the owner.

1. Who operates this application

Fleebee Statement Downloader is operated by E-Songa Ltd for the account owner's internal Fleebee workflow. It is not offered as a public email-reading or file-storage service.

2. Google user data accessed

The application requests the Gmail read-only OAuth scope. It may access only the information needed to identify and download expected statement attachments, including:

  • Gmail labels used to select bank-statement messages;
  • message identifiers, sender address, subject, date, and attachment metadata;
  • PDF attachment content for messages matching the configured label, sender, subject, and file rules.

The application does not request the ability to send email, delete messages, edit message content, or change Gmail settings.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to locate expected bank-statement messages, validate their PDF attachments, avoid duplicate downloads, and save those attachments to private storage for review and processing by the owner.

4. Storage and protection

  • OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored in access-restricted files on computers controlled by the owner.
  • Downloaded PDF statements are stored locally in protected Fleebee directories controlled by the owner.
  • Downloads use temporary files and are finalized only after successful validation.
  • Minimal operational records may retain a Gmail message identifier, attachment identifier, filename, cryptographic hash, processing time, and success or failure status to prevent duplicates and diagnose errors.
  • Gmail passwords are never requested or stored by the application.

5. Sharing, transfer, and advertising

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for targeted advertising. It is not transferred to unrelated third parties and is not used to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Downloaded statements remain in private local storage until the owner deletes them or the Fleebee statement workflow removes them under its retention rules.
  • Duplicate-prevention and operational records remain while needed to operate the downloader and may be deleted by the owner.
  • OAuth tokens remain until the owner revokes access or removes the application.
  • The owner can stop the downloader and delete its local credentials, tokens, records, and downloaded files at any time.

7. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's Google access at any time from Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future Gmail access. Locally stored files can then be removed directly from the owner's computers.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Fleebee Statement Downloader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Security and incidents

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect credentials, tokens, metadata, and downloaded attachments. If unauthorized access is suspected, the owner can disable the downloader, revoke Google access, and remove or replace the locally stored credentials.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's Gmail access, processing, storage, or retention behavior changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
